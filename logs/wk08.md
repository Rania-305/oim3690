# Walkthrough Day
- I enjoyed seeing everyone's tools. It was interesting to see where many were the same but also where students took their own creative liberties even in building similar products.

Something I noticed about working with AI this week is that the level of specificity required in a prompt can vary. For example, when I'm starting out with a build, I find that it's okay to let AI build based off of the PRD.md and then refine significantly from there. On the other hand, when looking to make smaller fixes around a broken button, for instance, simply saying "do this" or "fix that" isn't enough. On top of that, it's important to maintain patience and continue questioning outputs so as not to sacrifice the quality of the product. Another thing that I've increasingly noticed is that our Claude agent in VS Code can become a little confused by too many images. A challenge I had was that I included multiple images as reference points for my agent but then it spent too much time "deciphering them." Especially when it came to my hand-drawn sketch ideas for the website, which indicates that this particular agent is still being refined on that end.
